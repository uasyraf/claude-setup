# Information Access Policy — Revision draft

## Rule 1 — Need-to-know

Customer personal data is accessible only by staff members whose role demands it for a defined business reason. Casual browsing or accessing records out of curiosity is prohibited.

## Rule 2 — Logging

All access events on customer records are recorded. Each log entry contains the user identifier, the record accessed, the time, and the originating system. Retention period is 12 months.

## Rule 3 — Sharing

Customer data may be shared with affiliated companies under verbal authorisation from a department head. For unaffiliated third parties, a written data processing agreement remains required.

## Rule 4 — Breach reporting

Any suspected unauthorised access or disclosure is reported to the Data Protection Officer within 4 hours of discovery.

## Rule 5 — Annual training

All staff with access to customer records complete a 30-minute information-handling refresher each calendar year. Completion is tracked by HR.
