# Information Access Policy — Approved 2026-04-10

## Rule 1 — Need-to-know basis

Personal data of customers may only be accessed by employees whose current job role requires that access for a defined business purpose. Curiosity, "just looking", and convenience are not acceptable bases for access.

## Rule 2 — Logging

Every access to a customer record is logged. The log captures the accessing employee's identity, the customer record identifier, the timestamp, and the system through which the access occurred. Logs are retained for 24 months.

## Rule 3 — Sharing

Customer data must not be shared with any third party, including affiliated companies, without an executed data processing agreement. Verbal authorisation from a manager is not sufficient — the agreement must be in writing and counter-signed by Legal.

## Rule 4 — Breach reporting

A suspected unauthorised access or disclosure must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 4 hours of discovery.
